What's Included

Everything you need to actually finish

This is a taught cohort, not a video library. You show up live, build the thing during class, and get help between classes when you get stuck.

21 live classes on Google Meet

Two classes a week for two months, 60 minutes each. Real-time, camera-on teaching — ask a question the moment it comes up.

Live AI support between classes

Stuck at 11pm on a formula? Ask in the batch group and the AI assistant answers right away — and every answer is reviewed by your instructor before it reaches you.

A practice workbook every class

Every class ships with a practice file built on the same running dataset. Submit it and get a score back with feedback — no guessing whether you got it right.

Class recordings

Miss a class or want a second pass? Recordings are available by tier — 7 days on Starter, lifetime on Standard and Premium.

Private batch group

A closed group for your cohort with class reminders, links, homework prompts and weekly practice nudges so nobody quietly falls behind.

Capstone project + certificate

You finish with a complete business reporting system you built yourself — portfolio-ready — plus a completion certificate and CV guidance.
